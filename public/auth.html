Auth endpoints

Realistic sign-up, login, and password-change flows to use as an edge / WAF test target: throw SQLi/XSS at the form fields, watch how the edge handles the Set-Cookie session, and confirm authenticated responses bypass the cache. Login sets an HttpOnly session cookie; password change requires it.

In-memory only — accounts and sessions vanish on origin restart. Passwords are scrypt-hashed (node:crypto). Not real auth; don't store anything you care about.
